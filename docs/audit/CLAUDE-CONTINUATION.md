# TripTab audit continuation

The three interrupted Claude implementations were recovered from the local Claude Desktop conversation cache and replayed into isolated checkouts before integration. The commits retain that recovered code separately from completion work. Cached conversations and recovery scripts are private workspace evidence and are not published in this repository.

## Implemented behaviour

- Expense search and sorting, with payer, participant, category, receipt currency, date range and needs-review filters. Category, day and traveller spending views reuse the saved-row calculations.
- Traveller payment handles for PayPal, Monzo, Revolut and Wise, plus bank details and copying amount/reference. Connected travellers control their own details. Links open the provider; TripTab does not send or confirm money.
- Settlement reminders to connected debtors, sent only by the recipient or owner, checked against current balances, with a 24-hour limit and opt-out.
- Photo sharing through the PWA share target. One photo is staged locally for review before upload. The first-share route is bounded and works before the service worker controls the page.
- Personal archive/restore, leaving, transferring ownership and name-confirmed deletion. A shared holiday must have no other connected account before its owner can delete it. Retained travellers and history keep financial references intact.
- Traveller presence dates guide new splits. Unlinked, unreferenced travellers can be removed. A traveller may represent a couple or family with a weight of 1–20; saved expenses snapshot their weights.
- Budget progress, daily averages, a printable/shareable trip summary, and copying a saved expense onto following dates using current traveller weights and rounding.
- Email verification and password recovery, signed-in browser management and account deletion. Recovery disconnects old browser sessions and linked sign-ins. Deletion requires a current password or verified provider identity, and requires transferring/deleting owned holidays first.
- Notification settings for all/involved/no holiday updates, immediate/daily/in-app delivery, and settlement reminders. Daily summaries are generated by scheduled maintenance after 24 hours of accumulated updates.
- 52 currencies, with reference-rate currencies distinguished from manual-rate/bank-charge currencies. New ISK, JPY, KRW, VND and CLP receipts and payments use whole currency units. Storage remains hundredths for export compatibility. Existing calculation versions retain their balances; descriptive edits do not resplit historical receipts.
- Explicitly enabled offline capture on a device. The public offline screen stores new expenses and photos in IndexedDB, not a cached ledger. Pending entries can be inspected, edited or discarded. Reconnecting appends stable-ID entries to fresh server snapshots and retries conflicts, without overwriting other travellers' edits. Queues are scoped to the capturing account and limited to 50 entries, 10 MB per photo and 50 MB total photos. Shared photos are serialized as bytes for Safari compatibility. Device storage may be evicted by the browser; sync pending work before clearing site data.
- Undo after deleting an expense, payment or draft. Photos get a one-minute restoration hold before maintenance; exact restorations preserve trusted saved rounding/weight snapshots.
- Imports create new holidays after a preview and traveller selection. TripTab JSON preserves financial details, original currencies, bilingual items and saved calculation versions. Photos, drafts, chats, source history, account links and payment destinations are excluded. TripTab CSV preserves settlement amounts and exact shares as consolidated lines; a JSON export is preferable for item detail. Splitwise CSV supports single-payer net columns; ambiguous payers, refunds and mixed settlement currencies are rejected with an explanation. A basic CSV can use Date, Description, Amount, Currency, Paid by and semicolon-separated Shared by columns.
- An English, Spanish, French and German interface setting, privately stored per account and on the device. The translated interfaces currently cover navigation and common labels; advanced help, dynamic notices and some receipt tools retain English source copy. Receipt/traveller names are never translated by this interface setting.

## Deployment requirements

Apply migrations `0012_trip_lifecycle_email_recovery.sql` and `0013_account_preferences.sql` before deploying this application version. Migration 0013 gives existing sessions non-secret public IDs and permits erasing private account history only after the profile has become a deleted-account tombstone. Shared activity remains append-only. `/healthz` checks the new columns and tables.

Configure Worker secrets `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM` using a sender verified with Resend. Missing production configuration produces an explicit unavailable message; it never pretends to send mail. Loopback development uses console links. Verification/reset links contain their single-use token in the URL fragment; only hashed tokens are stored in D1. Reset links expire in one hour and verification links in 24 hours.

Keep the existing scheduled Worker enabled. It flushes daily notification summaries, removes expired unreferenced photos after Undo holds, and retries object deletion for deleted holidays/accounts. Deployment, production migrations, real email delivery and physical-device share-target/push checks are separate operations; local tests do not confirm their activation.

Account deletion removes sign-in credentials, sessions, private settings/history and contact/payment fields from the current shared traveller. The group retains financial entries, referenced photos and immutable history, including authored names and free-text notes. A minimal deleted-account tombstone and retained provider binding prevent automatic recreation of the old account.

Payment URLs and manual exchange rates still need the user's review. A recorded settlement and a provider link are separate actions. For unsupported rate currencies, enter a checked manual rate or actual bank charge. Whole-unit currencies use a new versioned calculation rule; old fractional balances remain visible rather than being rewritten.
